Official tournament rules · 2026

Dumb Luck InvitationalOne round a day. Play on your time.

Play four 18-hole rounds at Magnolia Pines, November 15–18. The lowest total score across all four rounds wins.

November 15–18, 2026All times EasternResults November 22

The cut: after Day 2

The top 10 players, plus everyone tied for 10th, advance. The cut uses combined strokes from Rounds 1 and 2. Everyone who makes the cut plays Rounds 3 and 4.

Your daily schedule

Each scoring day opens at 12:00 a.m. Eastern. Finish and submit all 18 holes by 11:59 p.m. Eastern that day, before the next midnight. There are no assigned tee times.

Playing and scoring

  • One counted round per day. Each tournament round is 18 holes. Lowest combined strokes wins; scores from the first two rounds carry into the final total for players who make the cut.
  • Finish before the daily deadline. Resume saved progress while your round window is open. A scored round cannot be restarted to improve your score.
  • Unfinished holes score 10 each. Quitting or leaving a started round unfinished does not erase its score.
  • Practice as much as you like. Practice is separate and never counts toward tournament standings.
  • First-place ties go to a playoff. Follow the tournament page for the organizer’s playoff instructions on November 22.

Fair play and technical issues

  • Use one account per person. No bots, modified game code, score manipulation, or exploiting glitches for an advantage.
  • Report disconnects or scoring problems to the tournament organizer promptly, before the round closes when possible. Include a screenshot if available. Any replay requires organizer approval.

Prizes

See the tournament’s Prizes tab for the published prize lineup. Winners must meet the published eligibility requirements and follow the organizer’s prize-claim instructions.
