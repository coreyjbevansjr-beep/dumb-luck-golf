Dumb Luck Golf

A free retro 8-bit side-view golf game that runs in your browser on phone, tablet or desktop. No download, no sign-up.

How to play

  1. Pick a club (driver, hybrid, iron, 9 iron, wedge or putter) and set your launch angle. Higher shots fly higher, roll less and catch more wind; lower shots punch through it and run out.
  2. Tap, click or press Space once to start the swing, again to set power, and a third time to stop the marker inside the sweet-spot brackets for a clean hit. Stop a touch early for a high, soft slice or a touch late for a low, running hook.
  3. Watch the wind, avoid sand, water and trees, and hole out in as few strokes as you can.
  4. Bad shots sometimes get a lucky bounce toward the pin. That is Dumb Luck.

Game modes

  • Front 9 and Back 9: two different nine-hole courses with deserts, cliffs, island greens, windmills and night holes.
  • Full 18: both nines in one round.
  • Daily hole: one special hole a day, the same for everyone, with its own leaderboard.

Leaderboards and unlocks

Post your score to the online leaderboard and unlock hats and ball trails for birdies, eagles, aces and daily streaks.
